# English recipes: «= id | Name», then «- step» and «! note» in the same order as the Greek.
= 1 | Spaghetti with mince
- Sauté the onion and garlic in the oil until soft.
- Add the mince and stir until browned and broken up.
- Deglaze with the wine, add tomato, tomato paste, salt, pepper (and 1 cinnamon stick / allspice if you like).
- Simmer for 30–40 minutes with the lid half open until thickened.
- Boil the pasta in salted water and serve with the sauce and grated cheese.
= 2 | Pasta with chicken, mushrooms & cream
- Cut the chicken into small pieces and sauté in the oil until golden. Set aside.
- In the same pan add butter, onion, garlic and the sliced mushrooms, until their liquid evaporates.
- Return the chicken, pour in the cream, salt, pepper and let it thicken for 5 minutes.
- Boil the pasta and toss it in the sauce with a little of the cooking water and the parmesan.
! Lighter option: light cooking cream (saves ~130 kcal per serving).
= 3 | Pasta with pesto
- Boil the pasta in salted water. Keep a cup of the cooking water.
- Drain and toss with the pesto and a little of the water to make it creamy.
- Serve with the cherry tomatoes cut in half and parmesan.
= 4 | Pasta with tomato sauce
- Sauté onion and garlic in the oil.
- Add the tomato, a little sugar (cuts the acidity), salt, pepper and simmer for 20 minutes.
- Finish with fresh basil. Serve with the boiled pasta and cheese.
= 5 | Carbonara
- Fry the bacon in small pieces without oil until crispy.
- Beat the eggs with the cream, parmesan and freshly ground pepper.
- Boil the pasta, add it to the pan with the bacon and turn off the heat.
- Pour in the egg mixture and stir quickly with a little cooking water (so it doesn't scramble).
= 6 | Pastitsio
- Mince: sauté onion, add the mince, deglaze with wine, add tomato, cinnamon, allspice and simmer for 30 minutes.
- Boil the pasta a little less than the pack says, drain and mix with 1 egg and some cheese.
- Béchamel: melt the butter, add the flour, stir for 1 minute and slowly add the hot milk, stirring until thick. Off the heat add 2 eggs, cheese, nutmeg.
- Layer in the tray: half the pasta, all the mince, the rest of the pasta, the béchamel and cheese on top.
- Bake at 180° for 45–50 minutes until golden. Let it rest 20 minutes before cutting.
= 7 | Chicken giouvetsi (orzo)
- Brown the chicken in the oil, add onion, tomato, salt, pepper, allspice and 2 cups of water.
- Put it in a clay pot or tray and bake for 40 minutes at 200°.
- Add the orzo and 4 cups of hot water, stir and bake another 25–30 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.
- Serve with grated cheese.
= 8 | Lentil soup
- Rinse the lentils, put them in a pot with water, boil for 5 minutes and discard the water.
- Add fresh water (3–4 fingers above the lentils), onion, garlic, carrot, bay leaf, tomato.
- Simmer for 40 minutes. At the end add the oil, salt, pepper and boil another 5 minutes.
- Serve with a little vinegar.
= 9 | Bean soup (fasolada)
- Boil the beans for 10 minutes and discard the water.
- Fresh water, onion, carrot, celery in small pieces and simmer for 1 hour.
- Add tomato, oil, salt, pepper and boil another 30 minutes until soft and the broth thickens.
! Soak the beans the night before.
= 10 | Chickpea soup (revithada)
- Boil the chickpeas for 10 minutes, skim and discard the water.
- Fresh water, sliced onions, the oil, rosemary and simmer for 1.5 hours (or in the oven in a clay pot).
- Finish with salt, pepper and lemon.
! Soak the chickpeas the night before (with a little baking soda).
= 11 | Fava
- Rinse the fava and boil it with the onion and water (3 times the volume of fava), skimming.
- Simmer for 40 minutes, stirring, until it breaks down. Salt at the end.
- Blend with half the oil and serve with the rest of the oil, lemon and spring onion.
= 12 | Baked giant beans (gigantes plaki)
- Boil the giant beans for 40–50 minutes until soft (not mushy).
- Sauté onion and carrot, add tomato, tomato paste, salt, pepper and parsley.
- Put the beans in a tray with the sauce and a little of their water and bake at 180° for 45 minutes.
! Soak the night before.
= 13 | Grilled chicken
- Butterfly the breasts into fillets (or pound them to even thickness).
- Marinate with oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper for at least 30 minutes.
- Cook on a hot grill or griddle for 5–6 minutes per side.
= 14 | Chicken with potatoes in the oven
- Cut the potatoes into wedges and put them in the tray with the chicken.
- Whisk oil, lemon, mustard, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper and pour over.
- Add 1 cup of water, cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes at 200°.
- Remove the foil and bake another 30 minutes until golden.
= 15 | Oven-baked burger patties (biftekia)
- Soak the bread in water and squeeze it.
- Knead the mince with grated onion, bread, egg, parsley, oregano, salt, pepper. Rest 30 minutes in the fridge.
- Shape 8 patties, brush with a little oil and bake at 200° for 30 minutes, turning halfway.
= 16 | Fried meatballs (keftedes)
- Knead mince, grated onion, soaked bread, egg, mint, oregano, salt, pepper.
- Shape small balls, flatten slightly and dust with flour.
- Fry in hot oil for 3–4 minutes per side and drain on kitchen paper.
! The oil counted is what the meatballs absorb, not all the frying oil.
= 17 | Giouvarlakia avgolemono (meatball soup)
- Knead the mince with the rice, grated onion, 1 egg, dill, parsley, salt, pepper.
- Shape small balls and drop them into a pot with 1.5 litres of boiling water and the oil.
- Simmer for 40 minutes.
- Avgolemono: beat 2 eggs with the lemon, slowly add broth from the pot while whisking and pour it back. Swirl the pot and turn off the heat.
= 18 | Soutzoukakia (Smyrna meatballs)
- Knead mince, soaked bread, egg, garlic, cumin, salt, pepper. Shape long meatballs.
- Brown them in the oven (200°, 20 minutes) or in a pan with a little oil.
- Sauce: oil, tomato, wine, a little sugar, cumin. Simmer 10 minutes, add the meatballs and cook 20 more minutes.
! Served with rice or mash (log them separately).
= 19 | Beef in tomato sauce (kokkinisto)
- Brown the meat pieces in the oil on all sides.
- Add onion and garlic, deglaze with the wine.
- Add tomato, tomato paste, cinnamon, allspice, salt, pepper and water to cover.
- Simmer for 1.5 hours (or 35 minutes in a pressure cooker) until tender.
! With pasta, mash or rice (log them separately).
= 20 | Pork with potatoes in the oven
- Cut the pork into portions and the potatoes into pieces, put them in the tray.
- Pour over oil, lemon, wine, garlic, oregano, salt, pepper and 1 cup of water.
- Cover and bake for 1 hour at 190°, then uncover and bake another 40 minutes until golden.
= 21 | Green beans in olive oil (fasolakia)
- Sauté the onion in the oil, add the green beans and toss for 5 minutes.
- Add tomato, potatoes in pieces, parsley, salt, pepper and water halfway up.
- Simmer covered for 45 minutes until only the oil is left.
! With feta and bread (log them separately).
= 22 | Briam (baked vegetables)
- Cut all the vegetables into pieces of the same size and put them in the tray.
- Add grated tomato, garlic, parsley, oil, oregano, salt, pepper and half a cup of water.
- Mix, cover and bake for 1 hour at 190°, then uncover for another 30 minutes.
= 23 | Gemista (stuffed vegetables)
- Hollow out the tomatoes (keep the pulp) and the peppers.
- Filling: onion, tomato pulp, rice, half the oil, mint, parsley, salt, pepper.
- Fill the vegetables to ¾, cover with their "lids" and put potatoes in between.
- Pour over the rest of the oil and 1 cup of water. Bake for 1.5 hours at 180°.
= 24 | Spanakorizo (spinach rice)
- Sauté the onions in the oil.
- Add the chopped spinach and let it wilt.
- Add rice, 2 cups of water, dill, salt, pepper and simmer for 20 minutes.
- Serve with lemon.
= 25 | Peas in olive oil (arakas)
- Sauté the onion, add peas, carrots and potatoes in pieces.
- Add tomato, dill, salt, pepper and water halfway up.
- Simmer for 40 minutes until only the oil is left.
= 26 | Moussaka
- Slice eggplants and potatoes, brush with oil and bake for 25 minutes at 200°.
- Mince: sauté onion, mince, deglaze with wine, tomato, cinnamon, simmer for 30 minutes.
- Béchamel: butter + flour, slowly the hot milk until thick, off the heat eggs and half the cheese.
- Layers: potatoes, eggplants, mince, eggplants, béchamel, cheese. Bake for 45 minutes at 180°.
! Here the eggplants and potatoes are baked with a little oil instead of fried, which is much lighter.
= 27 | Spanakopita (spinach pie)
- Chop the spinach, salt it and squeeze out its water well.
- Mix with spring onions, dill, crumbled feta, eggs, pepper and a little oil.
- Lay half the sheets, oiling each one, add the filling, cover with the rest of the sheets oiled.
- Score into pieces and bake for 1 hour at 180° until golden.
= 28 | Tyropita (cheese pie tray)
- Crumble the feta and mix it with yogurt, eggs, a little milk and pepper.
- Melt the butter. Lay sheets brushing with butter, add the filling, then more sheets.
- Score, pour the rest of the milk over and bake for 50 minutes at 180°.
= 29 | Chicken soup avgolemono
- Boil the chicken with onion, carrots, celery and 3 litres of water for 1 hour, skimming.
- Remove the chicken, strain the broth and cook the rice in it for 15 minutes.
- Avgolemono: beat eggs with lemon, slowly add broth and pour it back into the pot off the heat.
- Serve with the chicken shredded.
! Counts the whole chicken in the soup. Without the skin it's noticeably lighter.
= 30 | Vegetable soup
- Dice all the vegetables.
- Put them in a pot with 2 litres of water and simmer for 40 minutes.
- Add oil, salt, pepper. For a velouté, blend with a hand blender.
= 31 | Homemade french fries
- Cut the potatoes into sticks, rinse and dry them very well.
- Fry in hot oil in batches until golden.
- Drain on kitchen paper and salt.
! The oil counted (90g) is what the potatoes absorb, not all the frying oil.
! In an air fryer with 1 tbsp of oil it's ~200 kcal less per serving.
= 32 | Lemon oven potatoes
- Cut the potatoes into wedges and put them in the tray.
- Pour over oil, lemon, mustard, oregano, salt, pepper and 1 cup of water.
- Bake for 1 hour 15 minutes at 200°, stirring 1–2 times.
= 33 | Mashed potatoes
- Boil the peeled potatoes until soft.
- Mash with the butter and slowly add the hot milk until creamy.
- Salt, pepper and a little nutmeg.
= 34 | Rice pilaf
- Melt the butter and toss the rice for 1–2 minutes.
- Add 2 cups of boiling water (per 1 cup of rice) with the stock cube and simmer covered for 15 minutes.
- Turn off the heat and let it rest 5 minutes covered with a towel.
= 35 | Greek salad (horiatiki)
- Cut tomatoes, cucumber, pepper and onion.
- Add olives, the feta on top, oregano, salt and oil.
= 36 | Tzatziki

= 37 | Crêpes (basic)
- Beat eggs, milk, melted butter, sugar and salt. Add the flour gradually until you have a smooth batter.
- Let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
- Pour a ladle into a hot non-stick pan, spread, cook 1 minute per side.
! 1 serving = 1 crêpe. Log the filling (hazelnut spread, honey, ham-cheese) separately.
= 38 | Pancakes
- In a bowl mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
- In another bowl beat eggs, milk and melted butter. Combine gently (a few lumps are OK).
- Cook spoonfuls of batter in a non-stick pan until bubbles appear, flip for 1 minute.
! 1 serving = 3 pancakes. Log honey or syrup separately.
= 39 | Omelette with vegetables & cheese
- Sauté the vegetables in the oil for 2–3 minutes.
- Pour in the beaten eggs, the feta on top and cook on low heat until set.
= 40 | Porridge with banana

= 41 | Apple pie (milopita)

= 42 | Vanilla cake
- Beat the soft butter with the sugar until fluffy.
- Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
- Add the flour (mixed with baking powder) and the milk alternately.
- Bake in a buttered tin for 50–55 minutes at 175° (toothpick test: it should come out dry).
= 43 | Rice pudding (rizogalo)
- Boil the rice in 1 cup of water until absorbed.
- Add the milk (keep a little) and the sugar, simmer for 25 minutes, stirring.
- Dissolve the cornflour in the rest of the cold milk, add it and stir until thick. Add the vanilla.
- Divide into small bowls and sprinkle with cinnamon.
= 44 | Portokalopita (orange pie)
- Let the filo sheets dry and tear them into small pieces.

- Mix with the filo, spread in a tray and bake for 45 minutes at 180°.
- Syrup: juice of 3 oranges, 250g sugar, 1 cup of water, boil for 5 minutes. Cold syrup on the hot cake.
! The sugar includes the syrup (200g in the batter + 250g in the syrup).

= 47 | Chicken curry with rice

= 48 | Cheeseburger roll
- Crust: preheat the oven to 200° fan. On a lined tray spread the grated cheese into a rectangle of about 30×40 cm.
- Slice the onions into ½–1 cm rings and lay them in rows over the cheese. Sprinkle with smoked paprika, salt and pepper.
- Bake for 20–25 minutes until the cheese melts and browns and the onions caramelise. Leave the oven on.
- Mince: in a hot pan with the oil, sauté the mince for 4 minutes, breaking it up. Add tomato paste, a pinch of sugar, thyme, salt and pepper and cook 4 more minutes.
- Flip the crust onto the counter (with the paper). Spread the mince, top with the cheddar slices, a pickle slice on each, then ketchup and mustard.
- Roll tightly from the short side, wrap in foil like a sweet and bake for 2 minutes to melt the cheese inside.
- Let it rest 10–15 minutes, remove the foil and slice.
! The “cheese mix” can be e.g. gouda, edam, mozzarella, cheddar.

= 49 | Oven-baked soutzoukakia with bulgur
- Preheat the oven to 200° fan. Peel the onion and garlic.
- In a food processor blitz the bread into crumbs. Add the sliced garlic, 1½ tsp cumin and the mint and blitz again. Tip into a large bowl.
- Add the mince, 50 g olive oil, the egg, salt (5–6 g) and pepper.
- Grate the onion on the coarse side of a grater, squeeze out all the liquid with your hands and add it to the mince.
- Knead well, shape 20 soutzoukakia of ~40 g and place them on a non-stick tray. Drizzle with a little oil and bake 20 minutes on the top rack (they won't be fully cooked yet).
- Bulgur: in a bowl put the bulgur, 1–2 tbsp oil, 450 g boiling water and salt, cover with cling film and leave 20 minutes to absorb the water. Fluff with a fork.
- Sauce: in the food processor blend the chopped tomatoes, paste, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp sugar, 2 tbsp oil and 2 tbsp vegetable stock until smooth.
- Take out the tray, spoon the sauce over the soutzoukakia, pour 300 g water into the gaps and bake 20 more minutes.
- Serve the bulgur on plates with the soutzoukakia and sauce on top.
! Akis's tip: you can use pork mince instead of beef.
! The olive oil (122 g) is the total: 50 in the mince, ~2 tbsp for baking, ~1½ in the bulgur, 2 in the sauce.

= 50 | Oven-baked keftedakia with potatoes
- Wash and prepare the herbs and vegetables; keep the potatoes unpeeled. Preheat the oven to 200° fan.
- Potatoes: cut into wedges in a baking tray, add 2 tsp oregano, 3 tbsp oil, the lemon juice, salt and pepper, toss and bake 30 minutes (they won't be done yet).
- Meatballs (while the potatoes bake): in a food processor blitz the bread with ~10 mint leaves and the parsley into crumbs. Tip into a large bowl.
- Add the mince, milk, vinegar, egg, 1–2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp baking soda (optional), 2 tsp oregano, salt (6 g) and pepper.
- Grate the onion on the coarse side of a grater and spoon it into the mince, leaving the juices behind.
- Knead well and shape 28 meatballs of ~30 g.
- Take out the tray, place the meatballs on the potatoes, pour in 150 g water and bake 30 more minutes. Serve.
! Akis uses mince from beef brisket (regular beef mince). With lean mince it comes out lighter.
! The olive oil (58 g) is the total: 3 tbsp on the potatoes + ~1½ in the meatballs.

= 51 | Tray kebab
- Heat the oven to its maximum (230°) with top/bottom heat and fan. Line a tray with baking paper.
- In a food processor purée the onion, pepper, parsley and garlic cloves. Put it in a sieve and press with a spoon to drain the liquid well.
- In a bowl knead the mince with 1 tsp cumin, 2 tsp sweet paprika, the tomato paste, the drained mixture, 1 tsp chilli flakes, salt and pepper.
- Spread the mince on the tray into a ~34×26 cm rectangle (wet your hands if it sticks).
- With a spatula score parallel lines across the surface (the kebab pattern) and cut 8 strips of 3 cm.
- Dot with the butter in very small pieces and bake 20 minutes. Separate into 8 kebabs.
- To serve: in a pitta with strained yoghurt, 2 kebabs, tomato, onion, parsley and paprika. Fold the bottom up and roll.
! The calories are for the kebab only (as Akis gives them). The pitta (~165 kcal) and yoghurt count separately.

= 52 | Beef steaks with baby potatoes
- Take the steaks (black angus ribeye) out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead. Meanwhile boil the baby potatoes in their skins in salted water ~20 minutes, drain and let them cool a little.
- Heat the oven to 190° fan and a non-stick pan over high heat.
- Rub the steaks with salt and the oil and sear 1 minute per side. Add 2–3 thyme sprigs, 1 garlic clove and 1 tbsp butter, half a minute to melt.
- Move them with the butter and herbs to a tray and roast to your liking (thermometer in the centre): rare 45–52°, medium rare 55–57°, medium 60–63°, medium well 65–68°, well done 71°+.
- Sprinkle with pepper, rest 5 minutes and slice.
- Press each potato with your palm to crack it slightly. In the same (unwashed) pan fry them 2–3 minutes with thyme, the other clove and 1 tbsp butter until golden on both sides.
- Pour over a little juice from the tray, add salt and pepper, and serve with flaky salt on the steaks.

= 53 | Ribeye steak with red wine sauce
- Take the steaks out of the fridge 40–50 minutes ahead. Rub them with the oil and salt on both sides.
- In a smoking-hot pan sear 1–3 minutes without moving them, flip and cook 1 more minute.
- Add 3–4 thyme sprigs, 2–3 rosemary sprigs, the garlic cloves and the butter. Keep spooning the flavoured butter over the steaks for ½–4 minutes (depending on thickness). If it burns, lift the pan off the heat briefly.
- Move them to a plate with the butter and herbs to rest 4–5 minutes.
- Sauce: in the same pan over high heat add the wine with a little rosemary and thyme, let the alcohol cook off, add the chicken stock cube and the cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp water, and stir until the cube dissolves.
- Slice, sprinkle with flaky salt and pour over the sauce. Akis serves it with mashed potatoes and caramelised carrots.
! Akis's tip: if you have more than 2 steaks, cook them in batches so the pan isn't crowded.
! The calories don't include the mash and carrots.

= 54 | Mini burgers with caramelised onions
- Onions: slice thinly. In a hot pan add 30 g butter, the oil, the onions and salt and cook 5–7 minutes to caramelise. Add the balsamic glaze (with honey), toss, turn the heat right down and cook 2 more minutes. Set aside in a bowl.
- Patties: grate 30 g cold butter on the coarse side into the mince, add 10 g salt, mix and shape 12 small patties of 70 g.
- Halve the buns and toast the cut sides in a pan with 1 tbsp butter (in 2 batches), ½–1 minute. In the same pan cook the bacon ~1 minute per side.
- Cook half the patties, pressing them flat with a spatula: 2–2½ minutes on medium-high, flip, top each with 1 cheddar slice and ½ bacon slice and leave ½ minute to melt. Wipe the pan and repeat.
- Assemble: ketchup on the bottom bun, mayo on the top. Patty, caramelised onions, ½ tomato slice, a piece of lettuce, the lid, and a pickle slice on top with a toothpick.
! One “serving” here = 1 mini burger.

= 55 | Baked meatballs in tomato sauce with potatoes
- Potatoes: cut into wedges and sauté in a hot pan with 3 tbsp oil 4–5 minutes to colour. Put them in a ~28×36 cm tray.
- Sauce: in a bowl mix the paste, 400 g water, 1 tbsp oregano, 1 tsp sugar, 1 chopped garlic clove, salt and pepper. Pour over the potatoes with 1–2 tbsp oil and toss.
- Heat the oven to 180° fan. Purée the onion in the food processor, squeeze it well and put it in a large bowl.
- In the food processor blitz garlic, the bread (crusts off), parsley leaves, mint, 1 tsp cumin, 10 g salt and pepper. Add to the onion with the egg, vinegar, ketchup, 2–3 tbsp oil and the mince, and knead.
- Shape 8 patties of ~180 g and place them on the potatoes.
- Cover with foil and bake 40–45 minutes on the top rack. Uncover and bake 20–25 more minutes to colour.
- Serve with oregano leaves, feta and toasted bread.
! Akis's tip: you can prepare it the night before, keep it in the fridge and bake it the next day.
! The olive oil (91 g = ~7 tbsp) is the total for potatoes, sauce and patties.

= 56 | Beef stifado in the pressure cooker
- Peel the stifado onions (whole) and the garlic.
- Cut the beef (shank) into small pieces, salt it and toss with the flour to coat.
- In a hot pan with 2–3 tbsp oil brown the meat 3–4 minutes all over.
- In the pressure cooker with the rest of the oil sauté the onions 3–4 minutes. Add the meat and deglaze with the wine.
- Add the vinegar, paste, 1 cinnamon stick, 2 bay leaves, 3 allspice berries, the sugar, 150 g water, salt, pepper and the garlic crushed with your palm.
- Close the cooker (valve on 3). When the yellow ring shows, turn the heat right down, and from when the green ring shows cook 40 minutes.
- Release the pressure carefully. Serve with oregano leaves, pepper, a little oil, bread and feta.

= 57 | Beef curry with rice
- Chop the onion and garlic. Cut the beef into small pieces (across the grain), toss with the flour and plenty of salt.
- In a pot with 4 tbsp oil brown the meat 3–4 minutes. Add onion and garlic, then 3 tsp curry powder and 1 tsp ground ginger, and sauté 2–3 minutes.
- Add 2 bay leaves, the wine, 1 litre water and the honey. Once it boils, cover, turn the heat right down and simmer 1½–2 hours.
- Remove the bay leaves, add the zest of 1 lime and ¼ bunch chopped coriander.
- Basmati rice: in a small pot put the rice, 1 bay leaf, 2–3 rosemary sprigs, 500 g water, the peel of 1 lemon, 1–2 tbsp oil and salt. Once it boils, cover and simmer 12–13 minutes without stirring. Remove the aromatics and fluff.
- Serve the rice with the beef and sauce on top. Akis adds papadums, yoghurt with cumin, coriander and lime.
! The calories don't include the papadums and yoghurt.

= 58 | Osso buco with mustard sauce and mashed potatoes
- Heat the oven to 180° fan. Optionally snip the membrane around each osso buco slice in 3–4 places (so it keeps its shape). Rub with oil, salt and pepper.
- Roughly chop the onion; finely chop garlic, carrots, 3 thyme sprigs and 3–4 rosemary sprigs.
- In a shallow ovenproof pot brown the slices 2–3 minutes per side (in batches if needed). Add the vegetables and herbs and sauté 2–3 minutes.
- Add the wine, 600 g water and the chicken stock cube and bring to the boil. Lay a circle of baking paper on top, dipped in the liquid, cover and bake 2½ hours.
- Lift the meat onto a plate (any marrow left goes into the pot). Over medium-high heat add the cream, zest of 1 lemon, mustard and parsley and boil 3–4 minutes to thicken.
- Mash: boil the peeled potatoes in pieces ~15 minutes, drain 5–10 minutes and mash with the butter and salt.
- Serve mash, a slice of osso buco on top, sauce and parsley.

= 60 | Osso buco giouvetsi in the pressure cooker
- Optionally tie each osso buco piece round with string. Scoop the marrow out of the bones with a knife and keep it.
- In a large non-stick pan with half the oil sear the meat with plenty of salt 2–3 minutes, flip, add the marrow and sear 2–3 more minutes.

- In the pressure cooker with the rest of the oil sauté the vegetables with the sugar, thyme, salt, pepper and 2 bay leaves until coloured. Add the paste for 1 minute and deglaze with the wine.
- Add 1.2 litres boiling water, the meat and the chicken stock cube. Close (valve on 3) and from the green ring cook 30–35 minutes.
- Release the pressure, lift the meat into a covered bowl. Add the orzo to the cooker, close and cook 12–15 minutes.

= 61 | Baked burgers with rice
- Soak the bread slices in water 2–3 minutes. Grate the onion on the coarse side.
- Squeeze bread and onion well and put them in a bowl with both minces. Pour the vinegar over the mince.
- Add parsley, mint, 1 tbsp oregano, the grated garlic, the egg, 3 tbsp oil, 10–12 g salt and pepper, and knead 5–10 minutes until fluffy.
- Ideally leave it covered in the fridge 1–6 hours (or overnight). Shape 8–10 patties.
- Heat the oven to 180° fan. In a jug mix 1.2 litres boiling water, the stock cube, peel and juice of 1 lemon, the mustard, honey and 2 bay leaves.
- Spread the (long-grain) rice in a ~25×35 cm ovenproof dish, pour in the liquid, 3–4 tbsp oil, salt, pepper, and put the patties on top.
- Cover with foil and bake 40–45 minutes. Uncover and bake 15–20 more. Serve 2 patties with rice, oregano and pepper.
! The olive oil (85 g = ~6½ tbsp) is the total for patties and rice.

= 62 | Beef with chickpeas in the pressure cooker
- Beforehand: soak the chickpeas at least 6 hours in water with a pinch of salt. Drain and rinse.
- Cut the leek and carrots into half-moons, roughly chop onion and garlic.
- Cut the beef (chuck) into small pieces and toss with 2–3 tbsp oil, salt and pepper.
- In the hot cooker sauté the vegetables with 2 tbsp oil 3–4 minutes and remove. With the rest of the oil brown the meat in 2 batches, 7–8 minutes each.
- Put everything in the cooker: meat, vegetables, chickpeas, 1 tsp cumin, 1 cinnamon stick, paste, sugar, chopped tomatoes, 300 g water, salt, pepper.

= 133 | Beef topside with radish salad

= 134 | Smoked beef fillet
- Line the inside of an old pot and its lid very well with 2 layers of foil.
- Heat a griddle pan over medium-high heat. Rub the 2 beef fillets with the oil, salt and pepper, and sear 2 minutes per side.
- In the bottom of the pot put 300 g brown sugar, 2 cinnamon sticks, 15 peppercorns, 5 allspice berries and 5 cloves, and cover with foil pierced in a few places.
- Put a small rack inside and lay the fillets on it. Put the pot over high heat and, as soon as the first smoke appears, close the lid and lower to medium.
- Cook 15 minutes (medium to well done; for more well done, 4–5 more minutes). Lift them out carefully.
- Serve with mashed potatoes.
! The sugar and spices are only for the smoke — they aren't eaten, so they don't count. The mash for serving doesn't count. Akis's tip: you can also put the pot on the barbecue.

= 137 | Beef fillet with Béarnaise sauce
- Béarnaise: melt 200 g butter in a pan over medium heat until it starts to bubble.
